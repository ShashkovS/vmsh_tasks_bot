\problem

Find the perimeter of a parallelogram if the bisector of one of its angles divides a side into segments of $7$ cm and $14$ cm. Find all solutions.

\eproblem

\problem

Through each vertex of triangle $ABC$, draw the line parallel to the side opposite this vertex. Let the new three lines intersect at the points $A_1$, $B_1$, and $C_1$. Prove that the vertices of the triangle $ABC$ are the midpoints of the sides of the triangle $A_1 B_1 C_1$.

\eproblem

\problem

Let $ABCD$ be a parallelogram, and let $E$ and $F$ be the midpoints of the sides $BC$ and $AD$ respectively. Prove that $BF$ and $ED$ divide the diagonal $AC$ into three equal parts.

\eproblem

\problem

On the side $AC$ of the triangle $ABC$, the point $D$ is chosen so that $AD : DC = 1 : 2$. Prove that the triangles $ADB$ and $CDB$ have equal medians.

\eproblem

\definition

Let $ABCD$ be a quadrilateral, and $K$, $L$, $M$, and $N$ be the midpoints of the sides $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, and $AD$ respectively. We have proved that $KLMN$ is a parallelogram. It is called the \emph{Varignon parallelogram} of the quadrilateral $ABCD$.\\
